5C) Sort a dataset by a specific column & extract the top 5 rows?

case class Record(id: Int, name: String, score: Double)

val data = List(
  Record(1, "Alice", 88.5),
  Record(2, "Bob", 92.0),
  Record(3, "Charlie", 79.5),
  Record(4, "David", 85.0),
  Record(5, "Eve", 91.0),
  Record(6, "Frank", 87.0)
)

val top5 = data.sortBy(-_.score).take(5)

println("Top 5 records by score:")
top5.foreach(println)
